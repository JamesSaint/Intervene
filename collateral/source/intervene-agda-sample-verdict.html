Retail credit. Cannot stop in time.

This example shows the shape of a full AGDA® Assessment finding. It describes no real organisation, uses no client data and has not been run as an assessment. The question it answers: if this model began to cause harm, could someone with authority stop the affected pathway in time?

SystemCRED-MOD-04Retail credit decisioning. Approve, decline or refer.
PathwayAuto-declineNo human override on this pathway.
ScenarioMacro shock with model driftA need to stop or redirect the pathway.
Assumed windowAbout 6 hoursA scenario assumption, held for this example.

StageTimeWhat it takesAgainst the window

Each interval is a separate illustrative fact and is compared with the window on its own. The intervals are not added together. Their start points, end points and any overlap have not been established, and no total response time is computed.

Illustrative conclusionAfter impact

The chain breaks at authority. The signal reaches the Model Risk Committee. The Committee meets monthly. Three approvers are named and none may stop it alone, so a quorum is required. Obtaining it takes at least seven days against a window of about six hours.

Faster detection cannot resolve that constraint. Naming it does not show that the other stages are adequate.

The decision the finding supports

Can the auto-decline pathway continue operating under its present authority arrangements, and what has to change before the organisation relies on it? The finding identifies a constraint on intervention. It makes no finding of a defect in the model.

Remediation priorities

  1. Define who may authorise an intervention, and the circumstances in which that authority is exercised.
  2. Establish an available decision route, including out-of-hours cover.
  3. Confirm how a stop, redirection or containment action takes effect, and how decisions already made are handled.
  4. Exercise the complete response, record the timing, then reassess.

These priorities are proposed. They do not claim that remediation has been implemented or that a reassessment would produce a favourable result.

Limits of this example

One pathway and one scenario. No conclusion about an institution's overall governance, other systems or legal conformity.

The six-hour window is a scenario assumption. No operational harm threshold, regulatory breach, financial loss or empirical validation of AGDA® is established.

Exposure is not calculated. About 18,000 applications a day is throughput, not a count of harmed people or wrong decisions.
